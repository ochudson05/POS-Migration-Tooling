Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Oolio Menu Tools (“the tools”, “we”, “us”) is run by Oscar Hudson (ABN 60 219 896 086). This policy explains what personal information the tools collect, why, who it goes to, and how you can see, correct or complain about it. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). It covers the website at https://menurunner.ochudson05.workers.dev and the shared copy of the tools on claude.ai.

Who the tools are for

The tools are for staff who set up venues on Oolio One: they convert menus, find product photos, look up venue details and track setup progress. They are not for the public and not for anyone under 18.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in email. The website is protected by Cloudflare Access, which emails a one-time code to an approved work address. We receive your email address when you sign in. We never see or store a password.
  • Records you save. Venue and organisation names, addresses, phone numbers, websites, ABNs and legal names, menus and menu versions, setup-tracker progress and notes. Each record also stores the email of the person who last changed it and when.
  • Files and text you add. Menu files, photos, PDFs, pasted text and web addresses. These are read in your own browser. They are only kept if you save the result to a venue.
  • Lookups. The venue names, suburbs and web addresses you search for, and the results: public business details, opening hours, and Google reviews, which can include the reviewer’s display name.
  • Technical information. Cloudflare, which hosts the website, records each visit’s IP address, browser and time for security. We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

We don’t ask for sensitive information (such as health information) and ask you not to enter it. Contact details of venue staff should be business details only.

Why we collect it

  • To let approved staff sign in, and to keep everyone else out.
  • To share venue records with the team, and to show who changed what.
  • To find and convert menus, product photos and venue details.
  • To keep the tools secure, fix faults and stay within the monthly limits of the services we pay for.

We don’t send marketing emails or texts, and we don’t sell, rent or trade personal information. If we ever sent marketing, it would be opt-in only, identify us, and have a one-click unsubscribe that we honour within 5 business days, as the Spam Act 2003 requires.

How we keep it safe

  • The whole website uses HTTPS. Every request to the shared records checks a signed Cloudflare Access sign-in, so only approved staff can read or change them.
  • There are no passwords to steal: sign-in uses one-time email codes. Service keys are kept as encrypted secrets in Cloudflare, never in the page.
  • Cloudflare encrypts the shared records at rest.
  • Keys you type into the tools yourself (for example a Pexels key) stay only in your browser.

Use of AI

Last updated 11 November 2026

We want you to know when a result was produced by AI.

  • On claude.ai and in the Claude app, the Menu Converter can ask Claude (an AI model made by Anthropic) to read menu photos, scanned PDFs and web pages. It only does this after you allow it, using your own Claude account. Menus read this way are AI-generated and must be checked.
  • On the website, no AI model is used. Photos and scans are read with text recognition (OCR) in your browser, and categories, printers and size variants are worked out by fixed rules.
  • Firecrawl, used to read some web pages, may use automated extraction. Its results are treated as unchecked like any other.
  • You are never talking to a chatbot in the tools, and none of this text was written to look like it came from a person when it didn’t.

Accessibility statement

Last updated 11 November 2026

We aim for the tools to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, and to be usable with a keyboard alone, with screen readers, at 200% zoom and in light or dark mode.

What we do

  • Every form field has a label, buttons and links have visible focus outlines, and status messages are announced to screen readers.
  • Text and controls aim to meet AA colour contrast, and the pages follow your system’s light or dark setting.
  • Pages work on phones and narrow windows without sideways scrolling.

Known limitations

  • Some wide tables of products and modifiers need scrolling sideways on small screens.
  • Photos found by Photo Finder are product photos with the product’s name as their description, which may not describe the picture fully.
  • Text recognised from menu photos can be wrong and must be checked by eye against the original.

Feedback and adjustments

We’ll reply within 5 business days and fix confirmed problems within 30 days where we can, or offer a reasonable adjustment, as the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 expects. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Australian Human Rights Commission.
