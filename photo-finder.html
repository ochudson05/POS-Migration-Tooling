Menu Photo Finder

Load a product export from Oolio One. The app finds a free photo for each product, then saves them into a folder named after the venue, one image per product, each named exactly like the product.

1. Load the menu

Drop the Oolio One product export here (.csv or .xlsx), or

If the venue already sells online, its own item photos are used first, matched to your products by name. Works with Hungry Hungry ordering pages, at no cost. These photos belong to the venue, so use them only for that venue.

Better photos with Pexels or Pixabay (optional, free key)

Sources are searched in order: Pexels, Pixabay, Openverse (Flickr, Rawpixel and others, filtered to licences that allow business use), then Wikimedia Commons. When one runs out of free searches (Pexels: 200 an hour, Pixabay: 100 a minute, Openverse: 200 a day), it’s paused until its limit resets and the next one takes over automatically. Keys stay in this browser only.

2. Find photos

Each product shows up to 6 photos from different libraries, best match first. Click the one that suits the menu. The first is used if you don’t choose.

3. Save

“Save to a folder” asks where to put it, then creates the venue folder there. Each image is a JPEG named after its product, plus photo credits.csv listing where each photo came from and its licence.
