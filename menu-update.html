Menu Update

Compares a venue’s current Oolio One products with its new menu. It works out what to edit, finish, add and delete, and builds an update import that keeps every existing Product ID, so sales history, PLUs, printers and photos stay. It also gives you a delete list and a step-by-step checklist.

1. Files

The Oolio export is the venue’s product export from Oolio One. The new menu is an Oolio import file, usually made by the Menu Converter. The menu PDF is optional and is used to check the new menu’s prices.

2. Scope and settings

Only the ticked Oolio categories are compared. Products in other categories (often drinks, merch, vouchers, specials) are left exactly as they are. Categories with a match are ticked to start with.

3. New menu items

Each new menu item is matched to the Oolio product it replaces. A Quick fix only needs its name and/or price changed in Oolio, and the row shows exactly what changes. Check every row: pick a different product if the match is wrong. Only choose “Add as a new product” when nothing in Oolio could be renamed or repriced into it. A product marked hand-added has no printers or price-list prices. It was probably typed in by hand.

4. Oolio products not on the new menu

These are in the ticked categories but no new menu item uses them. Ticked rows go on the delete list. Only delete something that no longer exists in any form on the new menu: not renamed, a size, part of a combined item, or a modifier. Anything that still looks like a new-menu item starts unticked, with the reason. Untick add-ons, desserts and anything staff still sell.

5. Modifiers

New modifiers are matched to existing ones with the same price, and renamed. The rest are added. Old modifiers that aren’t matched are kept.

Modifier groups to build by hand

The import file can’t link modifiers to products. List the groups here and they go on the checklist. One per line, like: Garlic Bread: Add Piece, Add Cheese (optional) or Rump Steak; Scotch Fillet: Garlic, Mushroom, Pepper Sauce (pick one)
